The BMW M2 is the purest expression of BMW M performance in a compact package. Powered by a twin-turbocharged 3.0-liter inline-six, it delivers 475 horsepower to the rear wheels through your choice of a 6-speed manual or 8-speed automatic transmission. With its short wheelbase, aggressive styling, and track-tuned chassis, the M2 is a modern-day icon for driving enthusiasts.
