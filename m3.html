The BMW M3 is the legendary sports sedan that defined a segment. With a twin-turbo inline-six producing up to 510 horsepower, the M3 delivers supercar acceleration with four-door practicality. Available with a 6-speed manual or 8-speed automatic, and optional M xDrive all-wheel drive, the M3 is the ultimate daily driver for enthusiasts.
