The BMW i8 was a revolutionary plug-in hybrid sports car that proved sustainability and performance could coexist. With its striking butterfly doors, carbon fiber Life Module construction, and futuristic design, the i8 became an instant icon. The combination of a 1.5L turbocharged 3-cylinder and an electric motor delivered 400 horsepower and all-wheel drive capability. Though production ended, the i8 remains a design landmark that shaped BMW's electrified future.
