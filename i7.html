The BMW i7 is the all-electric flagship of the BMW lineup. Combining the luxury and presence of the 7 Series with zero-emission driving, the i7 offers an unparalleled executive experience. The M70 xDrive variant delivers 520 horsepower and 720 lb-ft of torque. Inside, a 31-inch rear entertainment screen, crystal controls, and executive lounge seating redefine automotive luxury.
