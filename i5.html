The BMW i5 represents the future of the iconic 5 Series. As a fully electric executive sedan, the i5 delivers instant torque, whisper-quiet operation, and zero emissions. The M60 xDrive variant produces 400 horsepower, making it one of the quickest 5 Series ever built. With a range of over 300 miles and DC fast charging capability, the i5 is ready for both daily commutes and long-distance journeys.
