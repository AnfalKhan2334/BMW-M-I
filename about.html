About BMW

Bayerische Motoren Werke AG, commonly known as BMW, is a German multinational manufacturer of luxury vehicles and motorcycles headquartered in Munich, Bavaria, Germany. Founded in 1916 as a manufacturer of aircraft engines, BMW has evolved into one of the world's most respected premium automotive brands.

The company's signature slogan, "The Ultimate Driving Machine," reflects its commitment to engineering vehicles that deliver exceptional driving dynamics, precision handling, and pure driving pleasure. From the iconic M Series to the electrifying I Series, every BMW is designed with the driver in mind.

History
1916

BMW founded in Munich, Germany, initially producing aircraft engines.

1923

BMW introduces its first motorcycle, the R32, featuring the iconic boxer engine.

1928

BMW enters the automobile market with the BMW 3/15, beginning its journey as a car manufacturer.

1972

The BMW M division is established, creating legendary performance vehicles.

2013

BMW i launched, pioneering sustainable mobility with the i3 and i8.

2026

BMW continues to lead with electrified M Performance models and autonomous driving technology.

Today, BMW operates production facilities across the globe and continues to push the boundaries of automotive engineering. The brand remains committed to its core philosophy: creating the ultimate driving machine while embracing sustainable mobility for the future.
